Financial Welfare Desk

Empower via Education & Relief

Every year, numerous talented and highly motivated students from Karak secure admissions across competitive departments at the University of Peshawar. However, some encounter sudden financial hardships that threaten their academic continuity. The Khattak Students Association (KSA) acts as a reliable safety net, ensures tuition coverage, and coordinates crucial sustenance resources.

By channelizing contributions into audited welfare brackets, KSA maintains a constant emergency pool. This fund is exclusively utilized to assist non-boarding hostel arrangements, procure curriculum material, or settle semester fee tallies for verified applicants facing severe monetary distress.

Did You Know?

100% of all receiving donations directly reach candidate tallies. Administrative overhead operations are fully managed internally by executive council pool collections.

Zakat Eligibility

We maintain fully separate registries for general voluntary contributions and Islamic Zakat funds. Deserving applicants are strictly verified by our welfare panel in absolute compliance with traditional asset guidelines.

Where Your Support Goes

We divide our mobilization resources into three highly structured departments to maximize ground-level efficiency.

Academic Funds

Semester Fee Assistance

Covers semester registration tallies, library membership requirements, and specialized degree resources for top-performing students experiencing financial setbacks.

  • Direct University Account Deposits
  • Curriculum Textbook Distribution
Emergency Medical

Blood Drives & Healthcare

Funds logistics for blood grouping drives, diagnostic emergencies at local teaching hospitals, and provides urgent medicine grants for students during illness.

  • 24/7 Patient Logistics Care
  • Therapeutic Medicine Procurement
Hostel Welfare

Sustenance & Boarding

Supports out-of-district scholars adjusting to campus hostels with meal subsidies, utility adjustments, and critical winter bedding arrangements.

  • Mess Bill Subsidies
  • Room Utility Adjustments
