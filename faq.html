🩸 Blood Drives & Emergency Medical Aid

You can request emergency blood immediately by clicking the "Get Urgent Help" button on our navigation menu, or by filling out the tracking form on the Contact Page. For critical, life-threatening delays, please call our 24/7 executive committee helpline directly. Our central network coordinates instantly with hundreds of active student donors across campus hostels to organize prompt transit to local teaching hospitals.

While our core structural programs focus on supporting registered students from Karak studying at the University of Peshawar, our medical emergency blood network is entirely humanitarian and open to anyone on campus in severe distress. We prioritize university students, faculty members, boarding scholars, and their immediate relatives during sudden hospitalizations.

Students can enroll by submitting their Name, Contact Number, Department, Hostel Room details, and verified Blood Group through our desk portal or via the general inquiry form on the Contact page. This critical database allows our secretariat desk to locate matches near the hospital when emergency replacements are requested.

📚 Academic Circles & Resource Distribution

KSA study circles are arranged by senior departmental scholars throughout each semester. Schedules, subject distributions, and hall locations are published on our announcements board. To secure a seat, contact General Secretary Adnan Nasim or state your current academic department and session in a message via our digital Helpdesk Form.

These resources are distributed to junior students at the beginning of each academic term, with priority given to candidates facing financial constraints.

We highly encourage senior students with exceptional academic tracks to lead specific subject revision sessions. If you want to contribute as a peer mentor, get in touch with our management desk during membership drives to align your availability with current student requests.

🏢 Hostel Support & General Welfare Desk

Securing accommodation at the University of Peshawar can be challenging for freshers. KSA offers direct support by guiding new students through the official hostel allotment process, providing temporary room sharing arrangements with seniors, and assisting with essential items like mattresses and study setups during their initial transit weeks.

Financial subsidies are allocated based on an objective assessment of need. Our welfare committee reviews formal requests submitted through the Donation page or Helpdesk. Applicants must provide evidence of current financial limitations, university transcripts, and hostel fee structures to verify eligibility for direct university payment coverage.
